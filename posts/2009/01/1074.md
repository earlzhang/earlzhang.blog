![](http://www.asus.com/999%5Cimages%5Cproducts%5C2320%5C2320_m.jpg)前些天刚买了一个[迪卡侬心率表](http://www.earlzhang.net/others/1050/)，就看到有更具创新精神的心率器——[华硕心率鼠标](http://www.asus.com/products.aspx?l1=27&l2=145&l3=658&l4=0&model=2320&modelmenu=1)。  

从上面官网网站的资料来看，这个鼠标应该也是采用红外线接触式的测量方法，并将检测结果在屏幕上通过软件输出。有兴趣的可以看这篇台湾网站的[评测](http://mag.udn.com/mag/digital/storypage.jsp?f_ART_ID=131992)。  

只是，为了运动、为了减肥监控心跳还有点必要，用电脑也算不得重体力劳动，有必要检测心跳嘛。  

查了一下，没找到这个鼠标的报价，若和普通无线光电相差不多，那还算有点卖点。要若是贵上一大截，那就还是当个概念“远观”一下比较好。  

以下是这个鼠标的简单属性介绍：  

> Heart rate measurement using Asus HeartTouching  
> Heartbeats and stress indicator are shown with an easy way. Take a breath following the respiration instructions to relax.  
>   
> 2.4GHz RF transmission with Dynamic power adjustment  
> Working distance is up to 30 feet. RF power will be adjusted based on the RF quality..  
>   
> High definition Laser  
> Laser resolution is up to 1200dpi. High definition laser provides precise and smooth tracking.  
>   
> Long battery life *  
> Ultra-low power consume. Battery life is up to 5 months with a AA battery.  
>   
> Mini size receiver stores away easily  
>   
>
